What is an itasha?

Itasha is a Japanese car-customization style that celebrates characters from anime, manga, games, and related pop culture through bold graphics and decorations.

SailorIoniq is my Sailor Moon-inspired take on that tradition — with a little electric magic. ⚡

A few questions

Why no seat covers?

The Ioniq 5 has seat-mounted side airbags and heated/ventilated seats. I don't want covers interfering with either the safety equipment or the comfort features.

Why no steering wheel cover?

It has a heated steering wheel, and I want to keep that feature working as intended. Plus, a poorly fitting cover can slip, so I'm leaving the wheel uncovered.

Is the car really Lucid Blue?

Yes! She's a Hyundai Ioniq 5 Limited AWD in Lucid Blue. The bright blue and magical gradients here are an artistic interpretation of her color.
